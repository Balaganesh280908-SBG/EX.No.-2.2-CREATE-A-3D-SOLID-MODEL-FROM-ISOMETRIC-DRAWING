# EX.No.-2.2 CREATE A 3D SOLID MODEL FROM ISOMETRIC DRAWING
## Date :

## AIM: 

To create a 3Dmodel from the given isometric drawing using fusion 360 tool. 

## REQUIREMENTS:
1. Autodesk fusion 360
2. Windows 10
3. 16 GB of RAM (integrated graphics recommend 1 GB or more)
4. 2.5 Mbps or faster download; 500 Kbps or faster upload 

## PROCEDURE

## Step 1 
Start the sketch in the top view. Create a slot of length 32 mm. Draw a 16 mm circle at the centre of each end of the slot. Extrude it by 8 mm and, on that base, draw a rectangle of 40 mm x 10 mm.

<img width="275" height="455" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/38d74513-f1f4-4e4d-9648-50f98c6c6c36" />

## Step 2 
Draw a 20 mm tangent from the centre origin point. Extrude it to 32 mm. On that base, create concentric circles of diameter 20 mm and 30 mm at a distance of 32 mm from the side of the rectangle. Draw two lines tangent to the circle. 

<img width="292" height="402" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/39a78612-1ec8-47f6-869a-f01180f78843" />

## Step 3 
Extrude it to a distance of -10 mm. Using that as the base, create two concentric circles of diameter 20 mm and 30 mm above it and extrude them by 10 mm. 
## Step 4 
Create a rib line of 12 mm. Once done, click Finish Sketch.

Go to Design, choose Drawing and select From Design. Place the front view, top view, side view and isometric view on the sheet.
